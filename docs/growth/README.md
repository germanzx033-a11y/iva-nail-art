## 1. Departmental Mission & Strategic Mandate
The Growth Department drives organic client acquisition for **IVA Nail Art NY** (`ivanailartny.com`), positioning the studio as the #1 destination for Russian Dry Manicures, Builder Gel (BIAB), Gel-X extensions, and bespoke nail art across Bay Ridge, Brooklyn (11209), and the greater NYC metropolitan area.

Unlike high-volume salons that rely on paid ads or coupon discounters, IVA Nail Art NY operates on an exclusive appointment model (maximum 3 clients per day). The Growth Department achieves full book capacity through:
1. **Technical Search Engine Optimization & Structured Data:** Providing search engines with rich, structured semantic knowledge via Schema.org JSON-LD (`NailSalon`, `BeautySalon`, `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`).
2. **Hyperlocal Dominance:** Capturing high-intent transactional search demand in Bay Ridge (11209), Dyker Heights (11228), Bensonhurst (11214), and Sunset Park (11220) with localized bilingual (EN/ES) landing architecture.
3. **Google Business Profile (GBP) & Google Maps 3-Pack Authority:** Maximizing local visibility under a compliant Service Area Business (SAB) model, continuous high-converting weekly posts, an 11-service catalog, and automated review acquisition.
